01 / Our story & approach

Real estate is personal.
Service should be too.

A property enquiry can turn into a home search, a lease, an investment discussion, or a commercial requirement. Each one begins with questions that deserve a clear and respectful response. The Reach combines the structure of business process outsourcing with the care needed in customer-facing conversations.

Our work can include first-response calling, buyer or tenant requirement capture, appointment reminders, CRM updates, and post-visit follow-up. The exact workflow is agreed with each client so the support team follows the right qualification questions, communication standards, and handover rules.
