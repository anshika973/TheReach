THE REACH — Real Estate BPO Website
===================================

Pages included:
- index.html — Home and real-estate BPO positioning
- about.html — Company approach and operating values
- services.html — Property enquiry services, workflow, and FAQs
- contact.html — Requirement enquiry form (front-end demo only)

Shared files:
- styles.css — Green brand palette, responsive layouts, photo cards, and mobile styles
- script.js — Mobile navigation, FAQ interactions, and demo form confirmation
- assets/reach-logo.jpeg — Supplied circular R/headset logo mark

Preview:
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Use the navigation to explore Home, About, Services, and Contact.

Brand and content:
- The visible company name is The Reach.
- Original page copy is focused on real estate BPO: property lead response, buyer/tenant qualification, inbound and outbound calling, site-visit coordination, CRM support, and post-enquiry customer care.
- Green colours complement the supplied logo mark.
- Call-centre and team photos are loaded from external image URLs; an internet connection is required for those photos and Google Fonts.

Before publishing:
- The contact form currently displays a browser confirmation only. It does not email or store submissions. Connect it to The Reach's business email, CRM, or a form backend before launch.
- Company phone, email, and physical address were not supplied, so none have been invented.
